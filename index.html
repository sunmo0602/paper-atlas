What Paper Atlas is

Paper Atlas is a geography channel on YouTube. Each episode opens a paper map and answers one "why" question about a place, such as why a million people live beside the driest desert on Earth, or why cattle outnumber people in half of Paraguay.

About the "paper atlas" app

The app named paper atlas in Google sign-in is the channel's own publishing tool. It is used only by the channel owner to manage the Paper Atlas YouTube channel:

  • upload finished videos and Shorts to the Paper Atlas channel,
  • set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and scheduled publish times,
  • read comments on the channel's own videos and post replies.

It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers or from any other Google account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Maps and sources

Maps are drawn from Natural Earth and NOAA ETOPO elevation data, both in the public domain. Photos, footage and music are credited in each video's description.
